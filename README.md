## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I restore my lifetime Pro purchase?
Open Vonimo, navigate to **Settings**, and tap **"Restore Purchases"**. Your lifetime access will be instantly verified with Apple StoreKit.

### Does Vonimo work offline?
Yes. All 1,200+ curated vocabulary words, audio pronunciations, and learning progress are stored locally on your device, allowing you to practice anytime without an active internet connection.

### How does the freemium access work?
Vonimo provides free access to foundational words across A1 and A2 levels. Unlocking the full CEFR library (A1 through C2) and custom practice modes requires a one-time lifetime Pro purchase.

## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026

At Vonimo, we believe that education apps should be distraction-free and respect user privacy.

1. **No Personal Data Collection:** Vonimo does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, identifiers, or usage analytics.
2. **On-Device Storage:** Your learning statistics, streaks, bookmarked favorites, and custom study settings remain strictly on your local device.
3. **No Third-Party SDKs or Ads:** The app contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking frameworks, or third-party analytics tools.
4. **In-App Purchases:** In-app transactions are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not store or process payment or credit card details.

For any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email listed above.
